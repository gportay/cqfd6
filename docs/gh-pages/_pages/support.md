## Get <mark>support</mark>

Have a question, or something does not work as expected? Search the [issues]
on GitHub, or open a [new issue] to get help.

## Report a <mark>bug</mark>

Report bugs in a [new issue] on GitHub. Tell what you ran, what you expected,
and what happened instead. Include the output of `cqfd6 --version` and
`docker version`.

## <mark>Contribute</mark>

cqfd6 is free software, licensed under the [GPL-3.0-or-later]. Contributions
are welcome! Fork the [repository], and open a [pull request] on GitHub.
